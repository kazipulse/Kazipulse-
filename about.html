About Kazipulse

Kazipulse is a survey and rewards platform designed to connect participants with opportunities to share their opinions and earn rewards from eligible activities.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a simple platform where people can participate in surveys, discover opportunities, track their earnings and engage with a growing community.

What You Can Do

📋 Take Surveys

Participate in available surveys and receive eligible rewards.

💰 Track Earnings

View your wallet balance and transaction history in one place.

🤝 Referrals

Invite eligible participants and track your referral activity.

🏆 Tournaments

Participate in community activities and follow tournament rankings when available.

How It Works

Create an account, complete your profile, explore available surveys and participate in eligible activities. Your account dashboard allows you to monitor your activity, wallet and transactions.
